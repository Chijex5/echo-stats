# Echo Stats

**Your music tells a story. Echo Stats helps you see it.**

---

## Introduction

Spotify tells you what you’re listening to now, but it gives little insight into the full story of your music history. For music lovers, our history holds memories, phases, and personal narratives—a deeper story that often gets lost in lines of playlists and recent plays.

**Echo Stats** turns years of Spotify listening data into insights, memories, and personal stories. It’s a premium way to rediscover forgotten favorites, track your evolving taste, and understand the soundtrack of your life.

---

## Why Echo Stats Exists

Music has always been deeply personal. It captures moments in time, moods, and experiences. However, as our listening history grows, these moments can fade into the background.

Echo Stats exists to ensure that:

- **Forgotten songs** are rediscovered and brought back into rotation.
- **Lost music memories** are given life again.
- **Changing music tastes** are celebrated over the years.
- **Hidden listening patterns** are uncovered and explored.

It’s more than analytics—it’s about reconnecting with the joy, emotion, and stories behind your music.

---

## Features

### Dashboard

Gain an overview of your listening trends with stunning analytics. From top artists to genre breakdowns, your entire music world is captured at a glance.

---

### Timeline Explorer

Step into a time machine for your Spotify history. **Travel back** to specific months or years, and relive the music that defined those moments.

---

### Song of the Day

Rediscover a forgotten favorite with a daily, hand-picked gem from your own listening history.

---

### Music Age

Calculate the "age" of your music library based on the release years of the songs you listen to. Are your tastes timeless, or do you lean modern?

---

### Listening Personality

Explore insights derived from your unique listening patterns. Are you an **Explorer**, a **Loyalist**, or something in between?

---

### Story Mode

Create and share **Spotify Wrapped-style stories** anytime, from any date range. Perfect for seeing your year-to-date trends or celebrating personal milestones.

---

### Historical Imports

Dive deeper into your relationship with music by importing your Spotify account’s archive. Unlock long-term insights with years of listening data.

---

## How It Works

1. **Connect Spotify**: Authenticate with your Spotify account to enable analytics.
2. **Sync Listening Activity**: Get real-time insights on your latest plays.
3. **Import Historical Archive** *(optional)*: Upload your Spotify listening history for comprehensive long-term data.
4. **Build Personal Timeline**: Visualize years of music listening habits in one place.
5. **Generate Insights & Stories**: Turn your data into stories, explore metrics like Music Age, and rediscover your unique soundtrack.

---

## Privacy

We take your data seriously:

- **Your data belongs to you**: Echo Stats ensures full ownership and transparency.
- **Read-only Spotify Access**: We don’t make changes to your Spotify account.
- **Data Deletion**: All data can be deleted at any time with no strings attached.
